HOW IT WORKS

From questions to next steps—with more clarity.

Healthcare journeys can involve multiple organisations and decisions. ClinicConsilium is designed to help people understand the navigation process one step at a time.

A simple pathway

Four steps to navigate with confidence

The exact process will depend on the service and provider involved. These steps describe the intended approach, not a guarantee that every workflow is currently available.

01 · Explore

Explore your options

Start with general health information and identify the type of care or support you are looking for.

02 · Prepare

Prepare your questions

Write down what you want to ask. Check which documents a provider needs before sharing anything sensitive.

03 · Connect

Contact the right provider

Confirm eligibility, availability, fees, referral requirements and the next steps directly with the organisation.

04 · Confirm and follow up

Confirm and follow up

Keep a note of agreed next steps and follow-up arrangements. Clinical decisions should remain with qualified professionals.

Prepare before you enquire

A little preparation can make conversations clearer

Before contacting a provider, consider what you need to know and what information is appropriate to share.

01

Your main question

Write down what you want to understand or arrange.

02

Relevant documents

Ask the provider which records or referral documents are required before sending anything.

03

Costs and timing

Confirm estimates, additional fees, waiting times and payment terms directly with the provider.

04

Privacy and contact

Use verified contact channels and avoid sending unnecessary personal or medical information.

Important boundaries

Your safety comes first

ClinicConsilium does not replace a clinician, emergency service or the provider’s own assessment. If symptoms are severe or urgent, seek emergency medical help immediately.

01

Medical decisions

Discuss diagnosis, treatment and medicines with a qualified healthcare professional.

02

Provider availability

A connection or enquiry does not confirm a booking or acceptance.

03

Questions or concerns

Contact us for platform-related questions or use the appropriate enquiry route.

A responsible approach

Clear expectations are part of better care.

We are building ClinicConsilium around useful information, transparent service boundaries and responsible connections. We do not replace qualified clinicians, emergency services or the decisions of patients and their care teams.

  • Confirm provider details, availability and fees directly.
  • Share personal or health information only through appropriate channels.
  • Seek qualified clinical advice for diagnosis and treatment decisions.
