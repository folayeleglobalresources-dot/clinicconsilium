Trust & security

Trust is built through clear commitments.

We are developing ClinicConsilium around privacy-conscious practices, transparent service boundaries and responsible information handling. We aim to be clear about what is in place today, what is being improved and what is still planned.

Our guiding principles

Security starts with responsible design.

These principles guide how we intend to build and operate the platform. They are not a claim of certification or proof that every planned control is already active.

01

Collect with purpose

Ask for information only when it is needed for a defined service or enquiry. Avoid collecting sensitive clinical details through general contact forms.

02

Limit access

Administrative and operational access should be restricted to authorised people, with permissions reviewed as the platform and team evolve.

03

Protect information

Use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards across hosting, form processing, storage and other services that may handle information.

04

Be transparent

Explain what information is requested, why it is needed, how it may be used and where a service is still under development.

05

Review partners

Prospective healthcare organisations should be assessed before being described as verified partners or presented as part of an active care pathway.

06

Improve continuously

Security is not a one-time task. Policies, configurations and operational processes should be tested, reviewed and updated over time.

Operational priorities

What responsible operation should include.

As the platform develops, these are areas that need documented procedures, implementation and review.

01

Secure configuration and secrets

Keep credentials and private keys out of public source code, use controlled environment configuration and review access to deployment settings.

02

Account and permission management

Use appropriate authentication, least-privilege access and timely removal of access that is no longer required.

03

Testing and recovery

Test forms, access restrictions, backups and recovery processes before introducing more sensitive information or workflows.

04

Incident response

Maintain a process to identify, assess, contain and respond to security incidents, including any required notifications.

05

Third-party oversight

Understand the role of hosting, analytics, email and other service providers, and assess them for the information they may process.

06

Policy and legal review

Review privacy notices, retention practices and operational procedures against applicable Nigerian data-protection and healthcare requirements.

Using the website safely

Know what to share—and what not to share.

General website enquiries are not a secure clinical record or treatment channel.

Appropriate for general enquiries

  • Questions about the platform and its current services
  • General feedback or accessibility comments
  • Professional or organisation partnership interest
  • Non-sensitive contact details needed for a reply

Please do not submit

  • Diagnoses, symptoms or detailed medical histories
  • Prescriptions, test results or medical records
  • Passport details, identity documents or financial credentials
  • Urgent medical requests or information about an emergency
