Built for trust

What we will and won't claim.

Clear information matters when people are looking for medicines. We will not present sample data as real stock or imply that a pharmacy is a partner before verification.

✓

Authenticity checks

Partner onboarding will include checks appropriate to the pharmacy, supplier, product and applicable Nigerian requirements.

◷

Transparent availability

Availability, price, collection and delivery information will be shown only when a partner can support and confirm it.

Prescription-only medicines will require the appropriate lawful prescription and professional dispensing process. This preview does not accept prescriptions.

Frequently asked

Before you enquire

Can I buy medicine here today?

No. Online ordering, payment, dispensing and delivery are not active. This page collects general interest only.

Are any pharmacies verified yet?

No pharmacy partners are currently confirmed. We will not label an organisation as verified until checks are completed.

Can you import a medicine for me?

International sourcing is not currently offered. Any future pathway will depend on product eligibility, licences, import rules and qualified partners.

What if I need urgent medical help?

This website is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department for urgent care.
