Frequently asked

Before you enquire

Can I buy medicine here today?

No. Online ordering, payment, dispensing and delivery are not active. This page collects general interest only.

Are any pharmacies verified yet?

No pharmacy partners are currently confirmed. We will not label an organisation as verified until checks are completed.

Can you import a medicine for me?

International sourcing is not currently offered. Any future pathway will depend on product eligibility, licences, import rules and qualified partners.

What if I need urgent medical help?

This website is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department for urgent care.
