Data subject request · NDPA 2023

Your personal data. Your rights.

Use this form to ask about personal information held by ClinicConsilium. Depending on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, object to processing, withdraw consent or request portability.

Do not use this form for urgent medical needs. Do not include diagnoses, medical records, passwords, national identification numbers or identity-document images. We may request proportionate identity or authority verification separately before acting on a request.

Submit a data protection request

Provide only the minimum information needed to identify and understand your request. A reference number will be displayed after the request is recorded.

Important information

  • This route is for data protection requests, not clinical enquiries or emergency assistance.
  • We will not disclose or change personal data until identity and authority have been checked where appropriate.
  • Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions or the rights of other people.
  • Keep the reference number shown after submission for follow-up.
