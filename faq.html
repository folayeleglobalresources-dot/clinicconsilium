About ClinicConsilium

What is ClinicConsilium?

ClinicConsilium is being developed as a healthcare navigation and coordination platform. Its aim is to make it easier to understand care pathways, find relevant information and connect with appropriate healthcare professionals and organisations.

Is ClinicConsilium a hospital, clinic or healthcare provider?

No. ClinicConsilium is a platform project, not a hospital or clinic. The website does not itself diagnose conditions, provide treatment, prescribe medicines or replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Who is the platform intended to support?

The platform is being designed to support people seeking care information and coordination, as well as qualified healthcare professionals and healthcare organisations interested in participating in an approved network.

Services, availability and accounts

Are all ClinicConsilium services available now?

No. The platform is being developed in stages. Each service should be labelled Open now, In preparation or Planned according to its actual status. Please check the Service Status page before relying on a service.

Can I create a patient or professional account?

Accounts are not yet available. You may submit a registration-interest request for manual review, but no account is created by submitting that request. We will communicate when account access is genuinely ready.

Does submitting a registration-interest form give me access?

No. The form records your interest for manual review only. It does not create a login, approve an account, establish a provider relationship or grant access to a dashboard.

Can I use ClinicConsilium to buy or source medicines?

Medicine sourcing is not part of the currently available public service. Do not submit medicine orders or payments through this website.

Healthcare providers and network participation

Can a doctor, clinic or healthcare organisation apply to join?

Eligible healthcare professionals and organisations may express interest through the relevant provider or partnership enquiry route. Submission is not automatic approval or confirmation of a listing.

How are healthcare providers reviewed?

Provider information and supporting credentials must be reviewed before a listing is approved for the public network. An application or enquiry alone does not mean that a provider has been verified or endorsed.

Does a provider listing guarantee an appointment or treatment?

No. A listing is not an appointment, treatment guarantee, clinical recommendation or confirmation of price and availability. Contact the provider directly to confirm the details relevant to your care.

Privacy, information and security

What information should I share through a general enquiry?

Share only the information needed to explain your question. Do not include diagnoses, detailed medical histories, medical records, identity documents, passwords or payment-card details in a general enquiry.

How does ClinicConsilium approach privacy?

ClinicConsilium is being developed with privacy and responsible information handling in mind. Please read the Privacy Notice and Trust & Security page for published information about data handling. Do not assume a feature is available simply because it is described as a future capability.

Can I send medical records through the contact form?

Please do not send medical records or sensitive clinical information through a general contact form. If a secure, purpose-built submission route becomes available, its instructions and availability will be stated explicitly.

Enquiries, appointments and safety

Does sending an enquiry book an appointment?

No. An enquiry is a request for information or follow-up. It does not confirm an appointment, eligibility, treatment, provider availability or a price.

Will I receive a reference number for my enquiry?

When an enquiry is successfully saved, the confirmation message displays its reference number. Keep that reference if you need to follow up with the team.

What should I do if I need urgent medical help?

Do not wait for a website response. Contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. ClinicConsilium is not an emergency service and does not dispatch ambulances or provide emergency clinical assessment.

How can I contact ClinicConsilium if my question is not listed?

Use the Contact page for platform-related questions and general enquiries. Please do not use the contact form for emergencies or confidential clinical records.
