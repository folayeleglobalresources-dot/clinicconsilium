Guidance for the questions that matter

Start with a topic below. Each guide is designed to help you prepare, know what to ask and understand what to confirm with a qualified professional.

01

Preparing for an appointment

Write down your main concern, relevant timeline, medicines and questions. Ask the clinic what documents are needed before your visit.

02

Comparing care options

Consider clinical advice, provider credentials, access, costs, location, risks and follow-up—not price or marketing alone.

03

Understanding estimates

Ask for a written breakdown of fees, exclusions, payment terms and how unexpected costs will be handled.

04

Protecting personal information

Share only what is needed, verify the recipient and use secure channels provided by the healthcare organisation.

05

Considering care abroad

Review clinical suitability, credentials, travel rules, recovery and a plan for follow-up before making commitments.

06

Knowing when to seek urgent help

Do not rely on online articles or website enquiries for emergencies. Seek local emergency medical help for severe or urgent symptoms.

Using health information well

Helpful information, alongside professional care

General health information can help you prepare questions, but it cannot assess your individual situation or determine the right treatment.

01

Check the context

Look for who published the information, when it was updated and which population it applies to.

02

Discuss decisions with a clinician

A qualified professional can consider your history, examination, tests and individual circumstances.

03

Treat updates transparently

Platform announcements should be clearly distinguished from medical education and confirmed service availability.

Our commitment

Clear information. Responsible healthcare connections.

We are building ClinicConsilium around useful information, transparent service boundaries and responsible connections. We do not replace qualified clinicians, emergency services or the decisions of patients and their care teams.

  • Confirm provider details, availability and fees directly.
  • Share personal or health information only through appropriate channels.
  • Seek qualified clinical advice for diagnosis and treatment decisions.
