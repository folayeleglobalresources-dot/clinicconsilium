HEALTHCARE NAVIGATION NOTES

Questions that help people make informed choices

The following educational themes can help people prepare for conversations with healthcare professionals. They are not individual medical advice.

How to prepare for an appointment

Write down your main concern, when it began, questions you want answered, medicines you take and any instructions already provided by a professional. Share details directly with the clinician using an appropriate channel.

How to compare care options

Look beyond convenience alone. Ask about qualifications, service scope, availability, likely costs, follow-up arrangements and how to raise a concern.

Understanding a written estimate

Check what is included and excluded, how long the estimate is valid, when payment is due, cancellation terms and how unexpected costs are handled.

Protecting your health information

Confirm who will receive a document, why it is needed, how it will be sent and how it will be protected. Avoid sharing records through public forms or unverified accounts.

Questions about treatment

Ask the treating professional to explain expected benefits, risks, alternatives, what happens without treatment and when you should seek further help.

Care across borders

Before travelling, clarify clinical suitability, provider credentials, travel requirements, recovery plans, local follow-up and who to contact if complications occur.
