# ClinicConsilium

**Tagline:** Trusted Healthcare. Without Borders.

ClinicConsilium is a Nigeria-first healthcare access platform concept, with a long-term vision to expand across Africa. This repository currently contains an interactive, responsive front-end website prototype.

## Preview locally

No build tools are required for the current prototype.

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or run a local static server.
3. Test the navigation, care-search preview, partner links and enquiry form.

## What the prototype demonstrates

- Public healthcare access landing page and care discovery form
- Doctor/specialist and hospital/clinic discovery concepts
- Local medicine availability and staff-assisted sourcing enquiry concepts
- Continuous patient health record and authorised record-sharing concepts
- International treatment and LifeSaver training enquiries
- Healthcare partner interest and general contact form
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile

## Important limitations

This is a **front-end prototype, not a live healthcare service**. Forms are demonstration-only and do not submit or store information. The provider directory, inventory, appointment booking, accounts, payments, video consultations, messaging, health records, integrations and verification workflows are not connected to a backend.

Do not enter or upload real patient information. Do not treat prototype listings as real providers, medicine stock, treatment availability or clinical advice.

Before launch, the product requires a secure backend, authentication and role-based access, private medical-document storage, consent and audit trails, clinical and pharmacy partner verification, operational support, security testing, privacy impact assessment and jurisdiction-specific legal/clinical/regulatory review. International medicine sourcing must not be offered until product registration, prescription, import, licensing, customs and destination-country requirements are reviewed by qualified professionals.

See `docs/` and `prisma/schema.prisma` in the separate production starter package for initial architecture, security and data-model planning. Those materials are not a complete production application.
