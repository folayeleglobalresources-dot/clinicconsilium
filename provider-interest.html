Protect patient privacy

This is not a clinical referral channel. Do not include:

  • Patient names or contact details
  • Diagnoses, symptoms or medical histories
  • Test results, prescriptions or medical records
  • Identity documents or confidential case information

A useful guide before you begin

What makes a helpful introduction?

Clear, concise information helps us understand the scope of your practice and the kind of relationship you may want to explore.

01 / ORGANISATION

Who you are

Use your official professional or facility name, location and a reliable business contact route.

02 / SERVICE SCOPE

What you provide

Describe your main services, service locations and whether care is delivered in person, remotely or through diagnostics.

03 / CREDENTIALS

How you are regulated

Identify the relevant professional council, licensing body or accreditation route. Formal checks happen separately.
