Safety first

The right help, at the right time.

ClinicConsilium is being developed to support healthcare navigation. It is not an emergency-response service and does not replace professional assessment, diagnosis, or treatment.

Contact us for general enquiries ↗
If this may be an emergency, act now. Contact local emergency services or go to the nearest appropriate emergency department. Do not wait for a response from ClinicConsilium or submit an online form.

Urgent symptoms

Seek immediate professional help for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, trouble breathing, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, or other suspected emergencies.

Professional advice

For diagnosis, medication, and treatment decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional who can assess your situation.

Protect sensitive information

Do not send medical records, prescriptions, identity documents, or detailed health information through public enquiry forms.

Help shape a more connected care experience.

Contact our team with general questions or partnership enquiries. Please do not send medical records or sensitive health details through the public contact form.
