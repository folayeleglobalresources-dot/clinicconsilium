SAFETY & RESPONSIBLE CARE

Your wellbeing comes first.

Clear guidance for using ClinicConsilium responsibly. We are building a healthcare access and coordination platform—not a replacement for qualified medical professionals, emergency care or clinical judgement.

01 / Urgent medical needs

If it may be an emergency, act now.

Do not wait for a response from ClinicConsilium. Website forms and messages are not monitored as emergency channels and may not be reviewed immediately. For Nigeria-specific emergency information, see the LifeSaver emergency information page. Contact local emergency services first.

01

Get urgent medical help

For severe, rapidly worsening or life-threatening symptoms, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

02

Follow professional advice

If a qualified clinician has given you an urgent care plan, follow it or contact the medical service responsible for your care.

03

Do not wait for an online reply

Do not use general enquiries, partner forms or social media messages to request urgent medical assessment or emergency assistance.

02 / Using health information

Use information to prepare—not self-diagnose.

Our public information is educational. It cannot assess your personal condition, establish a diagnosis or determine which treatment is right for you.

A

Speak with a qualified professional

Discuss symptoms, test results, diagnosis and treatment decisions with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

B

Be careful with medicines

Do not start, stop, share or change prescribed medicines based only on website content. Ask an authorised professional or pharmacist.

C

Check before travelling for care

Confirm provider credentials, clinical suitability, treatment risks, total costs and follow-up arrangements directly with qualified providers.

03 / Protecting your information

Keep sensitive health details private.

Use only the information a form specifically requests. General contact and partner-interest forms are not designed to receive confidential clinical records.

Please do not submitMedical records, diagnoses, symptoms, test results, prescriptions or treatment histories.
Keep identity documents privateDo not send passport details, national identification numbers, financial details or copies of identity documents through general forms.
Share only what is necessaryWhen contacting us, describe your platform question or partnership interest without including another person’s private information.
Use appropriate clinical channelsFor personal medical questions, contact your treating clinician or a suitable healthcare service directly.
04 / What ClinicConsilium is today

Clear boundaries while we build.

ClinicConsilium is in development. The current release focuses on public health information, education, general enquiries and partner-interest submissions.

—
