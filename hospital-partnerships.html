How the process may work

A clear, considered route to partnership.

We are taking a careful approach. The first step is to understand your organisation; any later stage will depend on review, readiness and agreed safeguards.

Start an organisation enquiry ↗
01

Share your organisation's interest

Provide your facility name, location, official contact details and a short introduction through the partnership enquiry route.

02

Initial review and clarification

Our team can review the information submitted and request relevant organisational details where appropriate.

03

Discuss suitability and safeguards

Potential scope, responsibilities, verification requirements, privacy and governance considerations would need to be discussed before any arrangement.

04

Agree any next steps

No listing, referral, appointment or data-sharing activity begins unless separately reviewed, agreed and formally enabled.

Who should get in touch?

For healthcare organisations ready to explore collaboration.

This pathway is intended for hospitals, licensed clinics, specialist centres and other eligible healthcare facilities. An authorised representative—such as a facility administrator, medical director or partnership lead—should submit the enquiry.

Helpful information to prepare

  • Registered facility or organisation name
  • Facility type, location and official contact details
  • Name and role of the authorised representative
  • Brief overview of services and areas of interest
  • Relevant registration or licensing details, if requested during review
Please protect patient privacy. Use the enquiry form for organisational and business contact information only. Do not submit patient names, diagnoses, symptoms, medical records, test results, prescriptions or identity documents.
