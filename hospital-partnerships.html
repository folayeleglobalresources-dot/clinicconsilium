Hospital & clinic partnerships

Connect your facility to a more coordinated care journey.

ClinicConsilium is developing a healthcare access and coordination platform designed to help people navigate care pathways and connect with qualified healthcare organisations. We are inviting hospitals and clinics to register their interest while partner onboarding and verification processes are prepared.

Register organisation interest ↗Explore partnerships

Current status: This is a partner-interest page. Submitting an enquiry does not establish a partnership, confirm facility verification, or activate referrals, appointments, patient records or integrations.

What partnership planning may cover

Facility identity & licensing

Establish an organisation profile and define a controlled review of facility registration, licensing and relevant contact details before any public listing.

Referral coordination

Plan clear enquiry hand-offs, referral ownership and communication expectations. Referral workflows will only be enabled after operational and clinical governance review.

Responsible information sharing

Design staff permissions, patient consent and audit trails before any identifiable patient information is exchanged between participating organisations.

Who should register interest?

Hospitals, licensed clinics, specialist centres and other eligible healthcare facilities with an authorized representative may use the organisation partnership enquiry route.
