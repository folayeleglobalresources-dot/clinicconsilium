What happens next

A clear, considered review.

  1. We receive your organisation's expression of interest when submissions are available.
  2. Our team may review the information and contact you for clarification.
  3. Any potential collaboration would require separate discussion, due diligence and written agreement.
Important to know

Interest is not approval.

Do not describe your organisation as a ClinicConsilium partner, verified facility or referral destination unless a formal arrangement has been confirmed in writing.

Privacy and safety

Keep this introduction general.

Please do not include patient names, medical records, diagnoses, test results, prescriptions or other identifiable health information. Use this form for organisation-level information only.
