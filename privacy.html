Privacy

Privacy Notice

This page explains the current limits of the ClinicConsilium website prototype. It is not a final legal privacy notice.

Important: The website is a prototype. Its demonstration forms do not transmit or store submissions. Do not enter real personal information, patient information or sensitive health data.

Current website behaviour

The public website presents project information and demonstration interface elements. There is no patient account, health-record store or live enquiry service in this prototype. Before any live collection is enabled, the project must confirm its legal operator, data-protection roles, authorised recipients, lawful basis, retention and deletion rules, security controls and contact route.

Third-party services

The prototype may load fonts and illustrative images from external providers. Your browser may connect to those providers to retrieve those resources. Their handling of connection data is governed by their own policies. A full inventory and review of third-party services is required before launch.

Your choices

Please do not submit personal or health information through demonstration forms. A reviewed notice describing applicable rights, contact details, processing purposes, recipients and retention periods must be published before a live service collects information.

Operator and updates

The legal operating entity and privacy contact have not yet been confirmed. This page must be reviewed and completed by a qualified Nigerian data-protection/legal adviser before production use.
