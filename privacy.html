Privacy & personal information

Your information deserves careful handling.

This privacy notice explains how ClinicConsilium approaches information shared through this website and its enquiry pathways. We aim to be clear about what information may be involved, why it may be used and the choices available to you.

Website privacy noticeReviewed 18 November 2026Nigeria-first platform
Important: ClinicConsilium is in development. This notice describes the intended privacy approach for the current website and must be read alongside the actual forms and services available. It is not a claim that every planned platform feature or privacy control is already operational. Do not submit medical records or confidential clinical details through general website forms.

01 / ABOUT THIS NOTICE

Scope of this notice

This notice applies to the public ClinicConsilium website and information submitted through its general enquiry and partner-interest pathways. It should be read together with any notice displayed beside a particular form or service.

ClinicConsilium is a healthcare access and coordination platform in development. The current release is focused on public health information, education, general enquiries and partner-interest submissions. Patient accounts, clinical consultations, medical-record exchange and medicine fulfilment are not represented as currently available services.

02 / INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE

Information you choose to share

Depending on the form you use, you may be asked to provide information such as:

  • Your name and contact details, such as an email address.
  • Your role or the type of enquiry you are making.
  • Professional, facility or organisation details when expressing partnership interest.
  • The contents of the message you choose to send.

Please provide only information that is relevant to your enquiry. Do not submit diagnoses, symptoms, medical records, test results, prescriptions, treatment histories, patient-identifying information, passport details or national identification numbers through general website forms.

Health information: General enquiry forms are not a secure clinical record or a channel for sending confidential medical information. If a future service requires health information, it should provide a separate explanation and appropriate safeguards before collecting it.
03 / WEBSITE OPERATIONS

Technical information

When you visit a website, hosting, delivery and security systems may process technical information needed to deliver pages, diagnose errors and protect the service. Depending on the configuration of those systems, this may include an IP address, browser or device information, request timestamps and server or security logs.

We do not use this notice to claim that a particular analytics, advertising or tracking tool is active. Any optional cookies or similar technologies, if introduced, should be explained in an appropriate cookie notice and reflected in the site's actual configuration.

04 / WHY INFORMATION MAY BE USED

How information may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used, where relevant and configured, to:

  • Read, assess and respond to the enquiry you submitted.
  • Route a partner-interest message to the appropriate team for review.
  • Communicate with you about the specific request you made.
  • Maintain website operations, prevent misuse and investigate technical or security issues.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Information should not be used for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate explanation and, where required, a valid legal basis or your permission.

05 / WHO MAY RECEIVE INFORMATION

Sharing and service providers

Information may need to be handled by people authorised to manage website enquiries and by service providers supporting functions such as website hosting, secure form processing, communications or security. The actual providers and their roles depend on the services configured for the website.

Information should be shared only to the extent reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, subject to appropriate confidentiality, access and contractual safeguards. We do not describe an enquiry as an automatic referral to a hospital, doctor, pharmacy or other provider. A partner-interest submission does not itself establish a partnership or verified status.

06 / SAFEGUARDS

Retention and security

Enquiry information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate operational requirements and applicable legal obligations. Specific retention periods should be confirmed and published when the relevant systems and processes are finalised.

Access to information should be limited to authorised people who need it for their work, and appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be applied. No website, database or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending confidential information through channels that are not designed to receive it.

07 / YOUR CHOICES

Your privacy choices and requests

Depending on applicable data-protection law and the circumstances, you may have rights to ask about personal information held about you, request access or correction, object to or restrict certain processing, or request deletion. Some rights may be subject to legal exceptions and identity checks.

If you contact us with a privacy request, describe the request without including sensitive health information or identity documents in the initial message. We may need to ask for proportionate information to confirm that we are responding to the right person.

08 / LOCATION OF PROCESSING

International processing

Some website or communications service providers may operate infrastructure in more than one country. Where personal information is processed or accessed across borders, the relevant arrangements should comply with applicable data-protection requirements and include safeguards appropriate to the transfer.

ClinicConsilium is being developed with Nigeria as its starting point. Any future expansion or cross-border care pathway should be accompanied by clear information about the organisations involved and how information is handled.

09 / YOUNG PEOPLE

Children and young people

This website is not intended to invite children to submit personal or health information independently. A parent, guardian or other person with appropriate authority should make enquiries on a child's behalf where appropriate. Do not submit a child's medical records or identifying health details through a general enquiry form.

10 / UPDATES AND CONTACT

Changes to this notice

As ClinicConsilium develops, its website features, providers and data-handling arrangements may change. This notice should be reviewed and updated when those changes affect the information collected or how it is used. The date at the top indicates the latest editorial review of this page; it does not by itself certify that every operational or legal review is complete.

Have a privacy question?

Use the contact page to send a website or privacy-related enquiry. Please do not include medical records, diagnoses, prescriptions, identity documents or other confidential information.
