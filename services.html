Our standard

Nothing is shown as verified until it has been checked.

Every professional, hospital and pharmacy must go through appropriate review against the relevant licensing and registration requirements before appearing as verified in a directory. Submitting a form never makes anyone a partner.

  • Licence and registration checks before any verified listing
  • Clear availability labels on every service
  • Careful handling of enquiries and personal information
