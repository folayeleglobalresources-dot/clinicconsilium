Future development

What we plan to introduce

These capabilities are planned—not currently available. They will require appropriate clinical, operational, privacy and security safeguards before launch.

Care coordination

We plan to help patients organise referrals, appointments and follow-up, so they know what to do next and which provider to contact.

Patient health records

We plan to give patients a secure place to keep important health information and choose when to share it with their healthcare professionals.

Care navigation tools

We plan to help patients find relevant healthcare services, compare available options and understand the steps involved in getting care.

Safety & trust

Clear information. Responsible care.

ClinicConsilium is a healthcare access and coordination platform in development. We do not currently provide clinical consultations, diagnosis, prescriptions or emergency response.

Read our trust & security approach ↗
✓Prospective professionals and facilities must undergo appropriate review before any verified listing.
✓Service availability is labelled clearly so planned features are not mistaken for live services.
✓General enquiry forms are not intended for medical records or sensitive health details.
✓Clinical decisions belong to qualified healthcare professionals and the patient-provider relationship.
Need urgent medical attention? ClinicConsilium is not an emergency service and does not monitor enquiries for urgent response. Contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

Not sure which pathway is right?

Send a general, non-urgent enquiry and we can help you identify the appropriate route. Please do not include diagnoses, test results or detailed medical records.
