About the project

Healthcare access, more connected.

ClinicConsilium is a Nigeria-first healthcare access and coordination platform concept, with a long-term ambition to support wider access across Africa through responsible partnerships.

Our direction

We are exploring ways to help people understand healthcare options, connect with qualified professionals and navigate care with clearer information. Any future service will depend on appropriate partners, operational readiness and professional review.

What exists today

This website is a front-end prototype. It does not provide medical care, diagnose conditions, prescribe medicines, book appointments, verify providers or store patient records. Demonstration forms do not transmit or save submissions.

Our principles

  • Respect people and protect their information.
  • Be transparent about what is available and what is still planned.
  • Work with appropriately qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Review legal, clinical, privacy and security requirements before launching services.

ClinicConsilium is not an emergency service. If you believe you may be experiencing a medical emergency, seek help from appropriate local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.
