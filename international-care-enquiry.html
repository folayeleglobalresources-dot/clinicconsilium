INTERNATIONAL CARE ENQUIRY

Share the general nature of your question

This enquiry route is intended for general questions about potential cross-border care navigation. It is not a clinical assessment or a treatment booking.

Keep the message general

You may describe the type of information you are looking for without including a diagnosis, detailed history, test results or identifying health information.

Confirm details independently

Treatment suitability, provider credentials, appointment availability, fees, travel rules and outcomes must be confirmed with the responsible professionals and official sources.

Protect your privacy

Do not attach passports, medical files, prescriptions or other sensitive documents to an initial enquiry. Use a secure, specifically confirmed channel only when appropriate.

Not for emergencies: if urgent medical help is needed, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest appropriate emergency facility.
