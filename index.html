A responsible approach

Clear expectations are part of better care.

We are building ClinicConsilium around useful information, transparent service boundaries and responsible connections. We do not replace qualified clinicians, emergency services or the decisions of patients and their care teams.

  • Confirm provider details, availability and fees directly.
  • Share personal or health information only through appropriate channels.
  • Seek qualified clinical advice for diagnosis and treatment decisions.
