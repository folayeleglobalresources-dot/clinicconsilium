What is ClinicConsilium? ＋

ClinicConsilium is a healthcare platform concept focused on care coordination, personal health records, medicine access pathways, and connections between patients and healthcare providers.

Can I bring records from a physical hospital? ＋

Not yet. Patient records and record-sharing workflows are planned concepts only and are not available on this prototype. Please do not submit or upload health information.

Will my health information be private? ＋

Privacy and consent are core design requirements. Before live launch, the platform must implement and validate appropriate access controls, secure storage and transfer, audit trails, and compliance with applicable Nigerian data-protection and health requirements.

Can ClinicConsilium source medicines internationally? ＋

The long-term plan includes exploring international medicine sourcing. Any service must follow applicable prescription, importation, product registration, licensing, and safety requirements. A request does not guarantee availability or supply.

Is ClinicConsilium available across Africa? ＋

The initial market is Nigeria. The architecture and partnerships are intended to support expansion into other African countries over time, subject to local regulations and operational readiness.

Can a clinic or hospital become a partner? ＋

The prototype demonstrates a possible future partnership journey, but its forms do not send or store submissions. No provider account or verification process is currently live.
