Continuity of care

Your healthcare information should travel with you.

Today, records stay trapped inside each facility. Our long-term goal is a permission-controlled health record you own and can share, so a new doctor never has to start from zero.

  • You decide who sees what, and can withdraw access
  • Every access is recorded and traceable
  • Clinical entries are attributed to qualified professionals

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is ClinicConsilium?

A healthcare access and coordination platform, starting in Nigeria. We connect people with healthcare information, providers and pathways. We do not diagnose, prescribe or treat.

Can I book an appointment or buy medicine now?

Not yet. Today you can read health information and submit enquiries and partnership applications. Appointments, medicine services and patient accounts are planned for later phases.

How is my information protected?

We collect only what an enquiry needs and store it securely. Please keep detailed medical information out of public forms. See our privacy page for details.

I am a doctor, hospital or pharmacy. How do I join?

Submit an interest or partnership form. Our team reviews every application, and verification is completed before any public listing.

Is this an emergency service?

No. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services right away.
