Questions

What to know before getting started.

Can I book an appointment on this website now?

This is an early website prototype. The forms demonstrate the intended journey but do not make appointments or contact a provider.

Does ClinicConsilium store my medical records?

No real medical records should be uploaded to this prototype. Secure accounts, consent management, private document storage and governance must be implemented before handling patient data.

Can ClinicConsilium source any medicine internationally?

No. Any future sourcing workflow must check medicine registration, prescription requirements, pharmacy and supplier authorisation, import permits, customs and destination-country rules. Some requests may not be lawful or suitable to fulfil.

Is this an emergency or diagnostic service?

No. The website does not diagnose, treat emergencies or replace professional care. If someone may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.
