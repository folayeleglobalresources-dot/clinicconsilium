A few answers

Questions, answered clearly.

Learn what ClinicConsilium is building, what is available today and how to take part.

What is ClinicConsilium?

ClinicConsilium is a healthcare access and coordination platform being developed to help people navigate health information, qualified providers and care pathways, starting in Nigeria.

Can I book an appointment or buy medicine now?

Not yet. The current release focuses on public health information and selected enquiries. Appointments, medicine access, patient accounts and clinical services are future development areas.

Can I submit medical records through the website?

Please do not send medical records, diagnoses, test results, passport details or other sensitive information through general enquiry forms.

How can a healthcare organisation partner with ClinicConsilium?

Use the relevant professional or organisation interest pathway. Submissions are subject to review and do not automatically establish a partnership or verified listing.

Is ClinicConsilium an emergency service?

No. ClinicConsilium does not provide emergency response, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
