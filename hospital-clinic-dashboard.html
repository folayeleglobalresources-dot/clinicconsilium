Non-negotiable safeguards

✓
Organisation isolationA facility cannot browse another facility's patients or internal operations.
✓
Least-privilege accessPermissions are limited to the user's role and assigned responsibilities.
✓
Consent and lawful basisClinical information is disclosed only through an authorised legal and care pathway.
✓
Audit trailSensitive access and administrative changes must be recorded and reviewable.
