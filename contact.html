Before you write

Help us route your enquiry responsibly

Please include only the information needed to understand your message.

01

State the purpose

Tell us whether your message concerns the platform, provider participation, partnership or accessibility.

02

Use official contact details

Provide a way for the team to respond and confirm your organisation’s official details where relevant.

03

Protect sensitive information

Do not include patient records, identity documents, payment-card information or urgent medical requests in a general enquiry.

Need help with your next step?

Contact us for platform-related questions. Do not use website enquiries for urgent medical needs or send confidential clinical records through general forms.
