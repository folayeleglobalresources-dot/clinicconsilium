A quick note before you send

Your privacy and safety matter.

This form is for general questions and non-clinical enquiries only. It is not a secure channel for sharing health records or requesting medical care.

  • Do not submit patient records, test results or diagnoses.
  • Do not include passwords, payment details or identity document numbers.
  • For urgent medical help, contact local emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department.

For a more specific request, you can also visit our provider interest or international care enquiry pages.
