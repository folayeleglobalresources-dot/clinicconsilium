Healthcare network onboarding

Introduce your practice or healthcare organisation.

Submit your details and regulatory evidence for review. Submission does not create verified status, partnership, referral access or a public listing.

Network application

Use official professional or facility information. Required fields are marked *.

Professional registration, facility licence or accreditation evidence only. Never upload patient records or patient identity documents.
Privacy: Regulatory references and uploaded evidence are stored privately for authorised review. They are not displayed in the public directory. Do not submit patient or clinical information.

Review process

1. Submit details and evidence.

2. An authorised reviewer checks the application.

3. Accepted applications can be converted into unpublished directory drafts.

4. Credential verification and publication are separate decisions.

Evidence types

  • Professional registration
  • Facility operating licence
  • Accreditation

PDF, JPEG and PNG. Up to five files.

Important

This is not an emergency, patient referral or medical records channel. Application submission does not guarantee acceptance.
