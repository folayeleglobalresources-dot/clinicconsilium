LifeSaver · Emergency information

When every minute matters, act now.

This page provides general emergency information. It is not an emergency call centre, medical assessment or ambulance service.

In Nigeria: call 112

112

112 is Nigeria’s national emergency number. Nationwide operationalisation is being rolled out, so access may vary by location or network. If you cannot connect, contact your state or local emergency service directly or go to the nearest emergency department.

If you are outside Nigeria, call the emergency number used where you are.

Tell the emergency operator

  • Your exact location, including the town, street, building and a nearby landmark
  • A callback number in case the call drops
  • What happened and when it happened
  • How many people need help and whether there is immediate danger
  • Any access instructions that will help responders find the location
Important service limitation: ClinicConsilium does not receive emergency calls, dispatch ambulances, coordinate emergency responders or guarantee that an ambulance or other response will be available. This page is information only.

While help is being arranged

  • Protect yourself and others from immediate danger, such as traffic, fire, violence or unsafe structures.
  • Follow the emergency operator’s instructions.
  • Provide first aid only within your level of training or as directed by the emergency operator.
  • Do not move someone with a serious injury unless staying there presents an immediate danger.
  • Do not give food, drink or medicine to someone who is unconscious.

If you cannot reach 112

Try again if it is safe, use another available phone or network, contact your state or local emergency management agency, or seek help directly from the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for ClinicConsilium to respond.

Emergency service availability and response arrangements can vary by location.

Official information: Nigerian Communications Commission — Emergency Services Number · Federal Ministry of Information — 112 operationalisation update.
