Our participation principles

Professional standards matter.

We want every provider-facing relationship to be grounded in accuracy, accountability and respect for patients.

A

Accurate information

Keep organisation names, service descriptions, locations, eligibility details and contact information current.

B

Credentials for review

Be prepared to provide relevant professional registration, licences or organisational details through an appropriate review process.

C

Transparent communication

Explain fees, referral steps, availability and service limitations clearly. Avoid guarantees of outcomes.

D

Respect for privacy

Handle patient information responsibly and use appropriate, authorised channels for confidential information.

E

Patient-centred practice

Support informed decisions and communicate in a respectful, accessible and non-misleading way.

F

Clear service boundaries

Make it easy for people to understand what your service can and cannot provide.

A responsible approach

Good connections begin with clear expectations.

ClinicConsilium is in development. An expression of interest is an introduction for consideration; it is not approval, verification, an active referral arrangement or a promise of patient volume.

  • Prospective providers and organisations may be asked for appropriate details and credentials.
  • Service information and availability must be confirmed directly with the provider.
  • Clinical decisions remain with patients and qualified healthcare professionals.
  • Do not send patient records or confidential clinical documents through general enquiry forms.

What happens next

A straightforward introduction process.

We will review the information shared and clarify whether there may be a suitable next step as the platform develops.

Introduce your organisation

Tell us who you are, your role, location and area of service.

Share relevant details

Provide service information and professional or organisational details requested through the appropriate process.

Initial review

We consider the information and may contact you to clarify your expression of interest.

Discuss possible fit

If there is a potential fit, we can discuss next steps. No partnership is active until formally agreed.

Important: ClinicConsilium is not currently operating as a live clinical referral marketplace. Submitting an interest form does not make an organisation a verified or active partner.
