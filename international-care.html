Look beyond the procedure

A complete care journey includes more than treatment

Before choosing cross-border care, consider clinical suitability, provider credentials, written costs, travel logistics and what happens after you return home.

01

Clinical suitability

Ask the treating clinician about expected benefits, risks, alternatives, recovery and whether remote review is appropriate.

02

Provider credentials

Verify the facility, relevant professional registration, specialty experience and the local regulator where applicable.

03

Written cost estimate

Request an itemised estimate and clarify what is excluded, including tests, medicines, complications, accommodation and follow-up.

04

Travel and entry rules

Check current visa, travel, escort and medical fitness requirements through official sources before booking.

05

Records and communication

Ask how records will be shared securely, what language support is available and who to contact with questions.

06

Recovery and continuity

Plan follow-up, complication support and communication with a clinician in your home country before travelling.

Questions to ask

Make an informed decision

Use these questions to structure conversations with the provider and your local clinician.

01

What are my alternatives?

Ask whether treatment locally, another treatment, or watchful waiting may be appropriate.

02

What is included in the estimate?

Request inclusions, exclusions, payment schedule, refund terms and a process for unexpected costs.

03

Who is responsible after treatment?

Confirm follow-up dates, emergency arrangements, record transfer and a named contact.

04

What if plans change?

Ask what happens if travel is delayed, treatment is postponed or you are not medically fit to proceed.

A responsible approach

Clear expectations are part of better care.

  • Confirm provider details, availability and fees directly.
  • Share personal or health information only through appropriate channels.
  • Seek qualified clinical advice for diagnosis and treatment decisions.
