Operating principle

Built around the care journey

Every enquiry deserves a clear next step.

The planned workflow helps teams route questions, coordinate qualified partners, and keep people informed — without presenting the platform as a substitute for clinical judgement.

Separate operating areas with a clear owner and completion criteria.

✉01 · Access & intake

Enquiries and first contact

Keep the first interaction organised, acknowledged and routed to the right team.

  • Monitor general and partnership enquiries
  • Track response ownership and due dates
  • Identify incomplete or duplicate submissions
  • Close the loop with a recorded outcome
＋02 · Care network

Hospitals and professionals

Build a dependable network of qualified providers before activating care services.

  • Review facility and professional applications
  • Verify identity, licence and relevant credentials
  • Record scope, location and service availability
  • Approve, decline or request missing evidence
▣03 · Service partners

Pharmacies and laboratories

Manage partner readiness without implying that an unverified provider is available.

  • Check registration and responsible contacts
  • Confirm service coverage and operating hours
  • Define fulfilment and escalation contacts
  • Review partner status periodically
⇄04 · Care coordination

Referrals and appointments

Coordinate requests between people and approved providers with clear hand-offs.

  • Record referral reason at an appropriate level
  • Track acceptance, scheduling and completion
  • Notify the responsible person of delays
  • Record the operational outcome
◷05 · Service quality

Response and issue management

Spot delays and recurring friction before they affect confidence in the service.

  • Review ageing enquiries and overdue actions
  • Log complaints and service interruptions
  • Assign an owner and target resolution date
  • Escalate unresolved or safety-related concerns
⌑06 · Governance

Security, access and audit

Protect sensitive information and maintain accountability for administrative actions.

  • Use role-based access and multi-factor authentication
  • Review access grants and consent status
  • Keep audit records for sensitive actions
  • Apply retention and incident-response procedures

Management review rhythm

A practical cadence for keeping work visible and accountable.

Daily control

Start-of-day review

  • Check new enquiries, overdue responses and urgent escalations
  • Confirm each open item has a named owner and next action
  • Review provider or facility applications awaiting evidence
  • Check service interruptions and unresolved complaints
Weekly control

Leadership and governance review

  • Review enquiry volumes, response times and closure reasons
  • Review partner verification and service coverage gaps
  • Check administrator access, audit exceptions and incidents
  • Agree improvement actions, owners and target dates

Intake management · 02

Turn every enquiry into an owned next step

Enquiry triage is the controlled front door for general questions and partner interest. It helps the team acknowledge requests, identify the correct route, prioritise safely and maintain a clear record of what happens next.

Operational workflow
ReceiveCapture the enquiry, timestamp, contact preference and submission source.
CheckReview completeness, duplicates, contact details and consent or notice requirements.
ClassifyChoose the correct enquiry category and identify any urgent safety concern.
AssignRoute to a named team member with a due date and next action.
ResolveRespond, record the outcome, close or escalate with an audit trail.

Enquiry categories and routing

Use the category to determine the responsible team, not to make a clinical decision.

CategoryTypical requestInitial routingRequired next step
General informationHow the platform works, access questions or public information.Customer supportSend an approved explanation or direct the person to the relevant public page.
Professional interestDoctor or qualified professional wishes to join the network.Provider onboardingAcknowledge interest, request required credentials through a secure process and begin verification.
Facility interestHospital or clinic asks about participating.Facility partnershipsConfirm facility details, registration, service scope and an authorised contact.
Pharmacy / laboratoryMedicine or diagnostic service partner enquiry.Partner operationsExplain onboarding requirements; do not promise fulfilment before verification.
International careRequest for information about treatment outside the country.International coordinationClarify the information requested and route to an approved coordinator; no outcome guarantees.
Complaint / safety concernComplaint, possible harm, safeguarding or urgent medical concern.Escalation ownerFollow the approved safety protocol immediately; emergency medical needs must be directed to local emergency services.

Priority and response handling

Operational priority is based on risk and time sensitivity; it is not a diagnosis or clinical urgency assessment.

Immediate escalation

Potential emergency or safety risk

Do not leave an urgent safety message in the routine queue. Follow the organisation's approved escalation protocol and direct medical emergencies to local emergency services.

  • Notify the designated escalation lead
  • Record only necessary facts
  • Document the action and time taken
High priority

Time-sensitive operational issue

Examples include a scheduled appointment at risk, a partner service interruption or a complaint requiring prompt review.

  • Assign an owner promptly
  • Set a clear response deadline
  • Escalate if the deadline is at risk
Standard

Routine information or interest

General enquiries and partner applications can follow the normal review queue with acknowledgement and transparent next steps.

  • Check completeness
  • Use approved response templates
  • Record the outcome and close reason

Queue quality checklist

Before an enquiry is marked complete, confirm the record is useful and appropriately limited.

Minimum record quality

  • Unique reference and received timestamp
  • Enquiry type, source and current status
  • Named owner, next action and due date
  • Response history and clear closure reason
  • Only the personal information needed to handle the request

Safe handling rules

  • Do not request full medical records through a general enquiry form
  • Do not give diagnosis, prescribing or treatment advice from the admin desk
  • Do not share enquiry details with an unverified partner
  • Use approved secure channels for sensitive information
  • Restrict access and retain an audit trail for status changes
i
Current implementation boundary: the existing queue is connected to general and partner enquiry records only. The routing matrix and priority guidance are operating instructions; response timers, assignment ownership and escalation notifications need dedicated workflow wiring before they can be treated as automated controls.
Coordinated care journeys · 03

Make every hand-off clear, safe and traceable

Care pathways describe how a person or partner moves through the ClinicConsilium service. Each pathway should show who is responsible, what information is needed, what the next hand-off is and when the process is complete.

Pathway design framework
i
Important distinction: these are proposed coordination workflows, not active clinical services. A pathway becomes operational only when qualified providers, verified partners, secure data handling, clinical governance and the relevant integrations are in place.

Required controls at every hand-off

These are the common standards that should apply across all pathways.

Patient rights

Identity, consent and transparency

Verify the person, explain what will happen, record the appropriate consent or other lawful basis and make sharing understandable.

Clinical responsibility

Qualified professional ownership

Diagnosis, prescribing, test interpretation and treatment decisions remain with appropriately qualified and authorised professionals.

Operational assurance

Traceability and escalation

Every hand-off needs an owner, timestamp, status, next action and escalation route, with sensitive access recorded in an audit trail.
