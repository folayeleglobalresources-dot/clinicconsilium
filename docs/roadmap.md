# Production roadmap

## Phase 0 — Discovery and governance
Confirm company operating model, patient population, clinical partner responsibilities, Nigerian regulatory advice, data controller/processor arrangements, emergency escalation and the first medicine categories/routes.

## Phase 1 — Public information website
Deploy the responsive brand website, CMS, educational content, partner interest forms and non-clinical enquiries. Add privacy/terms pages and accessibility checks.

## Phase 2 — Identity and directories
Build secure patient and partner accounts, language preferences, professional/facility verification, directory search and admin review. No real medical record storage until security review is passed.

## Phase 3 — Health record MVP
Patient timeline, secure uploads, record provenance, consent and access grants, export/download, audit log, and staff-assisted exchange with physical hospitals. Add FHIR/API integrations only with a participating facility.

## Phase 4 — Medicine access operations
Local availability requests, pharmacist/partner confirmation, sourcing case workflow, supplier verification, regulatory checklist, transparent quotes and milestone tracking. Start with staff-assisted handling and authorised partners.

## Phase 5 — Clinical and commercial integrations
Appointment booking, video consultations, approved payments, notification providers, pharmacy inventory and hospital integrations. Each launch depends on clinical governance, regulatory review and tested partner agreements.

## Phase 6 — Multi-country expansion
Country configuration for professional verification, medicine rules, privacy, currencies, payment methods, languages and emergency information. Enable each market only after local review.
